🚢 Cruise crew wheel

Practice real cruise ship situations with confidence ✨

🎡 Round 1

Guest Type
😤 Impatient guest
Situation
Their room isn’t ready yet.
You MUST use
• as soon as possible
• I understand your situation
🟣 A2+ Example

I understand your situation. Your room isn’t ready yet, but we’ll prepare it as soon as possible.

🔵 B1 Example

I completely understand your situation. Housekeeping is still preparing the room, but we’ll do our best to have everything ready as soon as possible.

🎡 Round 2

Guest Type
🤔 Confused guest
Situation
They don’t know where the yoga class is.
You MUST use
• main deck
• I can show you
🟣 A2+ Example

The yoga class is on the main deck. I can show you.

🔵 B1 Example

The yoga class is located on the main deck. If you’d like, I can show you where it is.

🎡 Round 3

Guest Type
😠 Angry guest
Situation
You are currently helping another guest at the reception desk. Suddenly, a different guest walks over and starts complaining because they’ve been waiting for a long time and want immediate attention.
You MUST use
• handle requests
• in order
🟣 A2+ Example

I’m sorry. We have to handle requests in order. I’ll help you soon.

🔵 B1 Example

I’m really sorry for the delay. I’m currently assisting another guest, and we have to handle guest requests in order. I’ll assist you as soon as I can.
