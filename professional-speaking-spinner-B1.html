🗣️ Speaking structure

  • Context: What was the situation?
  • Action: What did you do?
  • Challenge / Problem: Why was it difficult?
  • Result: What happened?
  • Opinion / Reflection: What did you learn?

I worked on a challenging legal case involving a contract dispute. I had to analyse documents carefully and explain the legal risks clearly to different departments.

It was challenging because there were many details involved, but I learned a lot from the experience.
